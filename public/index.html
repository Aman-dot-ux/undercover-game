📖 How to Play Undercover

🎭 1. The Secret Words

Every player receives a secret word. Most players (Civilians) receive the exact same word (e.g. MANGO). One or two players (Undercovers) receive a closely related word (e.g. PAPAYA). You won't know if you are a Civilian or an Undercover at first!

🗣️ 2. Giving Clues

In turn, each player provides a subtle, one-sentence clue about their word. Be careful: if your clue is too obvious, the Undercover can blend in. If your clue is too vague, others might suspect you!

💬 3. Discussion & Voting

After everyone has given a clue, the group discusses suspicious statements. Everyone secretly votes for who they believe is an Undercover. You cannot vote for yourself.

⚰️ 4. Elimination & Win Conditions

The player with the most votes is eliminated and their role is revealed.

  • Civilians Win: When all Undercover players are unmasked and eliminated.
  • Undercover Wins: When the remaining Undercovers equal or outnumber the Civilians.

🏆 5. Scoring

Civilians score 100 pts for team victory + 25 pts bonus for correctly voting out an Undercover. Undercovers score 150 pts for team victory + 50 pts survival bonus.
